# Code of conduct

Momoding contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and constructive.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent while still addressing real
impact, and make space for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are
not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior
violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact
shown on the repository profile. Do not use a public issue to report sensitive conduct.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the privacy and
safety of everyone involved.
