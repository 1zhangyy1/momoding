# Phosphor icon attribution

Momoding vendors a reviewed subset of the Phosphor 2.1 icon family instead of shipping the full
Compose icon package. The included Regular glyphs are Back, Close, More, Search, Add, Send, Stop,
Retry, Tasks, Settings, File, Folder, Terminal, Code, Warning, and Permission. Fill variants are
included only for Tasks, Settings, Send, and Stop.

The vector paths originate from [Phosphor Icons](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core), MIT
licensed, and were converted to Compose `ImageVector` source by the MIT-licensed
[`compose-phosphor-icon`](https://github.com/adamglin0/compose-phosphor-icon) generator. The
generated files remain mechanically equivalent to those upstream sources; Momoding only changes
their package and receiver names so the audited subset is local to this app.
