## How to work
- Specs and the official price come from the maker's own site (apple.com.cn, mi.com, huawei.com…): readable, and the reference everything else is compared with.
- Street prices: JD product pages are usually readable; deal and price-history sites (SMZDM / 什么值得买) show recent lows. Taobao, Tmall and Pinduoduo need a login or the app: don't try to get past that.
- Compare like for like: the same storage, colour group and version; say which variant each price is for and whether it is a promotion.
- Two or three sources are enough. If a shop walls you off, say so and use the official price as the reference.
- End with a short recommendation tied to what the person said matters (battery, camera, price), not a spec dump.
- Never add to cart, buy or sign in for the person.
