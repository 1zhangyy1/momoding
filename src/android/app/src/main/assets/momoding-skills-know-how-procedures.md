## How to work
- Official sources first: the national government service platform (gov.cn and its 政务服务 pages), the city's own government service site, and the department in charge (National Immigration Administration for passports and travel permits, the police for ID cards, 12333 social insurance, 12366 tax). Find them with a web search and prefer .gov.cn pages.
- Rules differ by city: use the person's city (USER.md) or ask once if it decides the answer.
- Answer in the order the person will act: where (online channel or office), what to bring, fee, how long it takes, what to watch out for. Say how current the page is.
- One or two official pages are enough; forums and news are only for what official pages leave out, and say so.
- Never book appointments or submit forms for the person; give the link.
